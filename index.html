Fluffy Pancakes

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 15 min · Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Preparation Steps

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk the milk, egg, melted butter and vanilla.
  3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry mixture and stir until just combined. A few lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake and cook until bubbles form, about 2 minutes.
  6. Flip and cook for another 1 to 2 minutes until golden brown.
  7. Serve warm with butter and syrup.
